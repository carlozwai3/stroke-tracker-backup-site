Privacy Policy — Stroke Tracker Backup

Last updated: October 14, 2026.

Purpose

Stroke Tracker Backup is an owner-operated, non-public utility for creating encrypted backups of the Stroke Performance Tracker database and saving them to an owner-designated Google Drive folder, with a backup status log.

Google account authorization

The utility requests the Google Drive drive.file OAuth scope. This permits access to files created by or specifically opened or selected for the utility rather than blanket access to every Drive file. The intended Google account is carlozwai3@gmail.com. Google OAuth credentials may be held securely in the private automation service to allow scheduled access. This informational website does not request Google sign-in.

Information processed

Backup archives may contain swimming training records, assessments, account identifiers, and authentication-related database records. These archives are encrypted before upload to private Google Drive storage. Backup metadata and status, such as timestamps, file sizes, and checksums, may be recorded in a designated Google Sheet.

Sharing and service providers

Data is not sold and is not intentionally published. Google Drive provides backup storage, GitHub Actions may execute the scheduled backup workflow, and Supabase hosts the source database. These providers process information according to their applicable terms.

Retention and security

Backups are kept until manually removed by the owner; no automatic deletion period is promised. Encryption helps protect backup contents, but no storage or transfer method is risk-free. Actual database recovery has not yet been independently verified.

Contact

For access or deletion questions about the backup utility, contact carlozwai3@gmail.com. This policy covers the backup utility, not the full Stroke Performance Tracker application.
